# Frequently asked questions

## How do I book a room for an event?
Open Bookings and choose New request. Type the request in plain words (for example "seminar hall kal shaam 6 baje, 2 ghante, 100 log, mic chahiye") and check the filled-in form, or fill the form yourself. The decision appears straight away, with the reason.

## What does "alternative offered" mean?
Nothing suitable was free inside your window, so the system found the nearest free slot or room outside it. The booking is held for you there.

## Why did I get a different room from the one I asked for?
The requested room was taken by a higher-priority or earlier request, did not have something you need (for example step-free access) or does not exist. The decision lists the reason.

## Can a club event move a class?
No. Only an approved institute event can move a regular class, and then only for Spectra week.

## What does "kal" mean in a request?
In a booking request "kal" means tomorrow. The system works out the weekday from the "today" setting and shows it, so you can correct it.

## How are periods numbered?
P1 is 08:00, P2 09:00, P3 10:00, P4 11:00 and P5 12:00. Lunch is 13:00 to 14:00. P6 is 14:00, P7 15:00, P8 16:00 and P9 17:00. P10 to P13 run from 18:00 to 22:00 and are for events only.

## I cannot teach on some days. What do I do?
As faculty, open Reschedule and describe when you are unavailable. The system proposes the smallest set of moves and an Admin approves or rejects it.

## Who approves bookings?
Decisions follow the priority rules automatically. An Admin can confirm a decision or reject it with a reason, but never for a request they submitted themselves.
